About Us

We are a global organization committed to ensuring that every child has access to education, healthcare, and a safe environment. Our mission is to create lasting change and empower communities to thrive.

Our Position on Child Labor

Creating safe spaces for every child to learn and thrive.

Eliminating the Worst Forms of Child Labour in Bangladesh

The Grambangla Unnayan Committee (**GUC**) stands unequivocally against **all forms of child labour**. Our focus is driven by the reality that millions of children, particularly in Bangladesh's informal urban sectors, are engaged in the **Worst Forms of Child Labour (WFCL)**—work that is hazardous, exploitative, and denies them their fundamental rights to education, health, and a dignified childhood.
